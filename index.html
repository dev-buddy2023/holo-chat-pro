✦ Mac Safari Extension

Send Webpages From Your Mac

Install the Holo Chat Pro Safari extension on your Mac, then send any webpage straight to your iPhone with one click and get an instant AI summary — perfect for long articles on the go.

⬇️ Download HoloChatProSafariPlugin.dmg

Install Guide

1. Download the .dmg and open it.
2. Drag SafariApp.app into your Applications folder.
3. Open Safari → Settings → Extensions → enable Holo Chat Pro.
4. Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.
